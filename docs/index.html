MedSeg-AI: efficient & accurate medical image segmentation

Polyp segmentation in colonoscopy. Five candidate algorithm families and five hybrid methods are benchmarked for accuracy per millisecond of CPU, robustness on another hospital's data, and calibrated uncertainty.
